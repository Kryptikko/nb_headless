# Progression
## Character
 - Unlocking ability slots 
  - primary - level 1
  - bonus (secondary) - level 3
  - passive 1 - level 6
  - passive 2 - level 14
 - Attribute boosts
  - on specialization (lvl 1, 6, 14)
 - Equipment
  - recipes for crafting unlock ~2 levels before the equipment required level, thus teasing and setting goals for the player

## Guild
 - guild level sets short term goals for the player
