# Recruitment
Recruitment introduces new characters to your roster called recruits.
A recruits attribute, personality and gear are hinted but not visible, they require cost (in time or action points) to reveal.


## Active
The player navigates to the recruitment area where hes presented with a set of characters.
The quality and quantity of characters is dependant on guild level (game progression).

## Passive
Events

## Stats hints
- level is revealed
- attribute - show the full sum of attributes but not individual stat distribution
- equipment - the quality and level of items is revealed but not the actual items
- personality - number of traits?
- abilities
